### 2026-08-24 — Ch. 3.1 Realized Returns

- Realized return is a holding-period cash identity (price in, coupons, reinvestment, price out), not YTM.
- Gross vs net vs return-on-capital: same P&L, different financing treatment and denominator.
- Reinvestment only enters if a coupon falls strictly inside the horizon.
- Not “coupons in hand.” Coupon is one term in the numerator; price change is the other (usually larger). Reinvestment is coupon interest, not a cost.
- Tuckman “realized/horizon return” marks the bond at P_T whether or not you sell. Accounting realized = coupons (+ reinvestment) plus price only if you actually sell; otherwise P_T − P_0 is MTM / unrealized.

### 2026-08-24 — Ch. 3.2 Yield to Maturity

- YTM is the single rate that discounts remaining CFs back to market price (IRR / quote). Not “return if held to maturity” — that needs 3.3’s two extra assumptions.
- Given the coupon schedule, y ↔ P is 1-1, so quoting yield is quoting price. Street uses yield because one number beats 2T spots.
- Premium/discount is coupon vs this bond’s own y: c = 100y → par; c > 100y → premium; c < 100y → discount. Yield > risk-free is a spread (cheaper / lower price), not a par test. “Yield + DF > RF → above par” mixes three different objects.
- Reframe: YTM is unit conversion (dollars of price → one flat rate). Same move as quoting a prepay path as CPR. It is not a forecast of holding-period P&L.
- Howard: “相对 price，yield 是多少” — yes. y is the rate implied by this price given this bond’s remaining CFs, not a promise of what you earn.
- Meaning of y: quote language (bid/offer can be in y; it is the price). Equals expected/horizon return only if coupons are reinvested at y and y does not change (3.3). Not E[r] in the forecast sense; 3.4: higher y is not automatically the better bid.
- Bond y changes whenever P changes or remaining CFs change. Drivers: curve move, issue spread vs curve, rolldown as tenor shortens. Flat static curve: y can stay put while price pulls to par.
- Rates ↓ ⇒ P ↑ ⇒ this bond’s y ↓. P and y are inverse labels of the same quote; the curve moves P, then y is re-read off P. Not “rates down, yield up.”
- Toy 2y 5% annual: year-1 CF = 5; year-2 CF = 5+100 = 105. Last payment is coupon plus par, not a mystery 105.
- Discount at y and compounding at y are inverses. You do not invest 100 unless the bond is par; you invest P. P grows at y to the reinvested CFs only if coupons are reinvested at y (3.3).

### 2026-08-24 — Ch. 3.3 Yield and Return

- y equals realized/horizon return only if (i) coupons reinvested at the initial y and (ii) ending y equals the initial y. Both are knife-edge. Hold-to-maturity drops (ii); only reinvestment remains.
- 30y 2.375s of 5/15/2051 bought at 2.343%: reinvest at 0% → 1.778%/yr; at 2.343% → 2.343%; at 5% → 3.207%. Same bond, same hold-to-maturity, return is not the quote.
