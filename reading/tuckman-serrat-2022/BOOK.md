## How the book is built

The preface splits the book into four blocks:

1. **Ch. 0** — markets, participants, a few medium-term themes (abundant-reserves policy, negative rates, liquidity).
2. **Ch. 1–6** — language and toolbox: arbitrage prices, rates/spreads, DV01/duration/convexity, multi-factor and empirical hedges.
3. **Ch. 7–9** — term-structure models: curve shape, derivatives pricing, relative value.
4. **Ch. 10–16** — markets in detail: repo, bond futures, short-rate derivatives, IRS, credit/CDS, MBS, options.

The 4th edition is written around the LIBOR → SOFR (and other RFR) transition.

## Suggested path

Start at **Chapter 0**, then 1 → 4 (discount factors, yields, DV01) before jumping to MBS/CDS. Chapters 7–9 can wait until the toolbox feels natural.

Ask with a chapter number or PDF page, e.g. “解释 Ch.1 law of one price” or “PDF 62 这段”.
